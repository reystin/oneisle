Before the fix (recorded in stronghold_harness_nondeterminism.csv):
StructurePlacementHarness.evaluateLegacy/evaluateModern ignored their seed parameter
and IslandDraft filled shapeSeed/biomeSeed with new Random().nextLong(), so the same
arguments produced different islands and distances (e.g. ancient_city 1052.37 vs 1489.40).
Fix: both methods now call setShapeSeed(seed) and setBiomeSeed(seed); Phase5StructureTest
does the same. STRUCTURE_REPORT_*.csv reruns are byte-identical.
